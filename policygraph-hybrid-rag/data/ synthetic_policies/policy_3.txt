Section 1: Health Coverage

Clause 1(a): Eligible Medical Expenses

This policy provides coverage for eligible medical expenses incurred by the insured person during the active policy period. Covered expenses may include hospitalization, medically necessary procedures, and other services specifically listed in the insurance schedule.

Clause 1(b): Hospitalization

Hospitalization expenses may be covered when admission is medically necessary and occurs at an eligible healthcare facility. The insurer may require medical records, invoices, prescriptions, and other documentation to assess the claim.

Section 2: Hospital and Treatment Benefits

Clause 2(a): Inpatient Treatment

Eligible inpatient treatment may include room charges, nursing services, prescribed medicines, diagnostic procedures, and medically necessary treatment received during an approved hospitalization. Payment remains subject to the applicable coverage limits and conditions.

Clause 2(b): Diagnostic Services

Diagnostic tests required for the evaluation or treatment of a covered medical condition may be eligible for reimbursement when ordered by a qualified medical professional. The policyholder may be required to submit the doctor's recommendation and original test documentation.

Clause 2(c): Emergency Treatment

Emergency medical treatment may be covered when immediate care is reasonably necessary to protect the insured person's health. The policyholder should notify the insurer as soon as reasonably possible following emergency admission.

Section 3: Claims and Documentation

Clause 3(a): Claim Submission

The policyholder must submit a claim within the period specified in the policy documents. Claims should contain accurate information regarding the treatment received, healthcare provider, dates of service, and amount claimed
