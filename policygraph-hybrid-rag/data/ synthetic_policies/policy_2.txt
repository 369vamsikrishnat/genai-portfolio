Section 1: Vehicle Coverage

Clause 1(a): Insured Vehicle

This policy provides coverage for the vehicle identified in the insurance schedule. Protection applies to accidental physical damage occurring during the active policy period. The vehicle must be used in accordance with the purpose declared by the policyholder when the policy was issued.

Clause 1(b): Collision Damage

Damage caused by accidental collision with another vehicle, object, or structure may be covered under the collision protection provision. The policyholder must report the incident and provide reasonable evidence describing how the collision occurred. Coverage is subject to the applicable deductible and policy limit.

Section 2: Theft and Loss

Clause 2(a): Vehicle Theft

Loss of the insured vehicle resulting from theft may be covered when the policyholder has complied with the applicable security and reporting requirements. The policyholder must notify the appropriate authorities and provide the insurer with a copy of the available incident report.

Clause 2(b): Theft of Accessories

Permanently installed accessories and eligible equipment may be covered when stolen together with the insured vehicle. Portable personal belongings left inside the vehicle are not covered unless specifically included under another applicable coverage provision.

Clause 2(c): Recovery of Stolen Vehicle

If a stolen vehicle is recovered, the policyholder must promptly inform the insurer. The insurer may arrange an inspection to determine whether the vehicle has sustained damage during the theft or recovery process.

Section 3: Claims and Repairs

Clause 3(a): Accident Reporting

The policyholder must report an accident to the insurer as soon as reasonably possible. Information regarding the date, location, vehicles involved, and circumstances of the incident should be provided to support the claim assessment process.

Clause 3(b): Repair Authorization

Repairs to significant vehicle damage should not begin before the insurer has had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the vehicle. The insurer may recommend or approve a repair facility based on the terms of the policy.

Clause 3(c): Total Loss Assessment

When the estimated cost of repairing the vehicle exceeds the applicable threshold, the insurer may classify the vehicle as a total loss. The settlement amount will be determined according to the policy terms, vehicle valuation provisions, deductible, and applicable limits.

Section 4: Exclusions and Conditions

Clause 4(a): Unauthorized Use

Damage occurring while the vehicle is being used for an undeclared commercial purpose or by an unauthorized driver may be excluded from coverage. The policyholder is responsible for providing accurate information about the regular use of the vehicle.

Clause 4(b): Policy Deductible

The policyholder must pay the deductible applicable to the covered loss before the insurer makes payment. Different types of claims may have different deductibles as specified in the insurance schedule.

Clause 4(c): Fraudulent Claims

Claims involving deliberate false statements, fabricated evidence, or intentional concealment of relevant information may be denied. The insurer may take further action where fraudulent activity is identified.
