
Section 1: Property Coverage

Clause 1(a): Covered Property

This policy provides protection for the insured residential property, including the main building, permanently installed fixtures, and eligible structures located on the insured premises. Coverage applies when the property is damaged by a covered event during the active policy period. Personal belongings located inside the residence may also be covered subject to the limits, conditions, and exclusions described in this policy.

Clause 1(b): Covered Events

Covered events include accidental fire, lightning, certain storm-related damage, and other sudden physical events identified by the policy. Damage must result from an unexpected event and must not be caused intentionally by the policyholder. The insurer may require evidence showing the cause and extent of the damage before approving a claim.

Section 2: Flood Protection

Clause 2(a): Flood Damage

Flood damage to the insured residential property is covered when flood protection has been included in the policy. Covered losses may include physical damage to walls, flooring, permanently installed fixtures, and other eligible property affected directly by flooding. The insured must take reasonable steps to reduce additional damage when it is safe to do so.

Clause 2(b): External Water Sources

Flooding caused by external water sources may be covered under the flood protection clause. Examples include water entering the insured premises because of heavy rainfall, overflowing water bodies, or surface water accumulation. Coverage remains subject to the applicable policy limits, deductibles, and exclusions.

Clause 2(c): Internal Water Damage

Water damage caused by a sudden and accidental burst of an internal pipe may be covered under the property protection provisions. Gradual leakage, poor maintenance, and damage caused by unresolved plumbing problems may be excluded unless specifically covered elsewhere in the policy.

Section 3: Claims and Reporting

Clause 3(a): Notice of Loss

The policyholder must report property damage to the insurer as soon as reasonably possible after discovering the loss. For property damage claims, notice should normally be provided within thirty days unless circumstances outside the policyholder's control prevent timely reporting.

Clause 3(b): Supporting Documentation

The policyholder may be required to provide photographs, repair estimates, receipts, ownership records, and other documents reasonably requested by the insurer. Failure to provide required documentation may delay claim assessment or affect the determination of coverage.

Clause 3(c): Inspection

The insurer may inspect the damaged property before authorizing major repairs. The policyholder should preserve damaged property and evidence of the loss where reasonably possible, except when immediate action is necessary to prevent further damage or protect people.

Section 4: Exclusions and Special Conditions

Clause 4(a): Intentional Damage

Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder or by a person acting with the policyholder's knowledge or permission is not covered. Loss resulting from fraudulent claims, deliberate misrepresentation, or intentionally concealed information may also be excluded.

Clause 4(b): Policy Limits and Deductibles

The insurer's payment for a covered loss cannot exceed the applicable policy limit. The policyholder is responsible for the deductible specified in the policy for each eligible claim. Where multiple coverage provisions apply to the same event, the insurer will determine payment according to the applicable limits, conditions, and exclusions.

Clause 4(c): Effective Coverage

Coverage applies only during the policy period stated in the insurance documents. Claims arising from events occurring before the effective date or after the expiration of coverage are not covered unless an applicable provision specifically provides otherwise.
